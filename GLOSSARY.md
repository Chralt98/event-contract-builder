# Glossary

## Forecast question unit

One Yes/No forecast question together with its optional condition (including its unmet disposition) and any variables with their allowed values.

## Forecast Contract

A reviewable forecast question with clarified terms and conditions, resolution sources, and resolution criteria. It gives forecasters a clear basis for assigning probabilities; it is not itself a probability forecast or a tradable market contract.

## Approved stage content

The content the user has approved for a stage and that is included in the
forecast export. A valid manual edit to already approved content updates it
directly.

## Pending model draft

Content prepared by the model, or copied from a model proposal into the draft,
that still needs the user's explicit approval in chat. Editing it does not
approve it.
