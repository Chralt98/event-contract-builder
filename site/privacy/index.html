Privacy

This page explains what the hosted Bleavit Foresight service stores, why it is used, and how you can delete it.

Information processed

The service receives the content in your explicit tool calls, which may include forecast questions, conditions, variable values, definitions, resolution sources and criteria, background information, and news. It also processes the record ID and timestamps needed to continue and expire a workflow. The MCP service does not request the full chat transcript. Your ChatGPT or Codex client may process conversation content under its own privacy terms.

Each workspace contains one specification and belongs to your Bleavit Foresight account. You can authenticate with Google or a one-time code sent to your email address. The service stores linked sign-in identities and internal account identifiers separately from forecast content. Workspace ownership uses the internal account identifier; source IPs are used only for coarse abuse limits, never to identify the owner of a specification. The application does not store raw IP addresses in D1. Cloudflare separately processes network and request metadata as described below.

If you choose the private feedback option, the feedback tool accepts only the text you submit. The service stores that text separately from Forecast Specifications with a random feedback reference and timestamps and an account identifier used to authorize deletion. This identifier can associate feedback with its account, but no forecast content, account details, contact address, or conversation history is attached automatically. Cloudflare may process technical request metadata as described below. Free-text can still contain personal data if you include it, so do not include personal, sensitive, or secret information. ChatGPT or Codex separately processes the conversation and tool call under its own privacy terms.

Purposes and legal bases

We use submitted content to provide the workflow you request, including validating, rendering, storing, retrieving, and deleting forecast specifications. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the performance of our service agreement for this processing (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We and Cloudflare may process technical request and error information to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service. Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis for this security and reliability processing is our legitimate interest in maintaining a secure and available service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use Forecast Specification content for advertising, analytics, profiling, or model training. Feedback follows the separate purpose below.

We use feedback that you deliberately submit to review and improve Bleavit Foresight. Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), subject to a documented balancing assessment. You can object to this processing using the privacy contact below. Support emails about feedback are used to respond and may inform service improvements.

Monthly and weekly usage statistics

When usage measurement is enabled, we count Bleavit Foresight accounts that complete at least one successful forecast tool call in a calendar month or calendar week (Monday–Sunday), using Europe/Berlin time. This helps us understand service adoption and plan its operation and development. The count covers connected clients together; it does not identify unique people or ChatGPT accounts. Preparation, feedback, sign-in, and visits to account settings do not count as forecast activity.

We also keep separate monthly and weekly totals of accounts that open the Export view after completing the required approvals and optional News decision, including choosing to skip News. This helps us measure completed workflows. Opening a preview with missing approvals does not qualify, and downloading a file is not required. Repeated openings or multiple forecasts count the account only once per period.

We use your existing internal account identifier and the current activity month and week, plus separate current-period membership for the Export milestone, solely to avoid counting your account twice in each statistic. This is personal data, even though the identifier does not contain your name or email address. We also record whether the usage notice has been provided and your objection preference. We do not store individual calls, exact activity times, tool names, forecast or conversation content, IP addresses, device identifiers, or OAuth tokens for this measurement. It adds no tracking cookies or browser storage. The membership is stored in the existing authentication database hosted by Cloudflare, subject to the provider arrangements described below, and is not added to Worker logs.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in measuring adoption of our own service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), subject to an assessment of necessity and the balance with your rights. We begin measuring an account only after providing this usage notice on its account or authorization page. OAuth access authorization is not treated as consent to analytics. We do not use these statistics for advertising, profiling, or decisions about individual users.

You can object to usage measurement at any time, without giving a reason and without affecting your access to the service. Use the “Exclude account” button in your account settings, or contact support@bleavit.com. An objection stops future measurement, removes your current membership, and adjusts all current monthly and weekly totals, including the Export totals. Deleting your account also removes that membership. Account settings show your preference and whether your account is included in the current month's and week's counts.

Only the current month's and week's account membership is needed. Past membership is removed by scheduled maintenance after the respective period ends, normally on the next 15-minute run, or replaced when the account is active in the new period. We retain only period/count totals without account references: up to 24 calendar months for monthly totals and 104 calendar weeks for weekly totals, including the current period. Access is restricted to the service operator. Past totals retain no account membership and are not adjusted for later objections. Your notice flag and objection preference remain with your account until deletion; you can change your objection preference at any time.

How information is used and stored

Submitted information is used to validate, render, and continue your forecast specification. The hosted service stores records in a Cloudflare Worker and D1 database. Sign-in with Google or a verification code sent by email establishes a Bleavit Foresight account; ChatGPT or Codex then receives separate OAuth authorization to use the MCP service. Specification IDs select records but do not grant access. The account that authorized the request must own each record. You can revoke connected MCP clients or delete your account and its forecast data from the account page.

Private feedback is stored separately from Forecast Specifications in Cloudflare D1. The plugin does not expose a way to read feedback records. Keep the feedback reference returned after submission if you may want to delete the record sooner.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides the Worker and database infrastructure and may process connection, request, and error metadata to operate and secure that service. The Worker code does not put forecast content or record IDs into its error messages. The D1 database was created with an Eastern Europe (EEUR) location hint; Cloudflare says a location hint does not guarantee an exact location or restrict all processing to that region. The Worker runs on Cloudflare’s global network. Cloudflare may process personal data in other countries under its data processing terms and applicable transfer safeguards. See Cloudflare’s Data Processing Addendum.

ChatGPT or Codex routes your explicit tool calls to the MCP endpoint; its handling of the conversation and account data is governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy information. If you choose to post a non-sensitive bug report on the public GitHub issue tracker, GitHub processes that information and the report is publicly visible. Do not post forecast content, record IDs, credentials, or personal information there.

If you choose the email option, the plugin cannot guarantee that its mailto link will open an email application; it may open in a browser. Any browser, email application, or provider that handles the draft processes it. If you send it, the support mailbox provider processes the message and sender address so we can respond and review your feedback. If you choose GitHub, you submit the issue directly to GitHub; it is public and governed by GitHub's terms and privacy information.

Retention and controls

Each account may keep one active draft and up to three compact archives. An archive expires 30 days after it is created; reading or exporting it does not extend that period. Active drafts keep the service’s existing expiry behavior. You can ask the MCP service to delete a specification immediately with delete_forecast_specification. Deletion is permanent. Deleting your account revokes its connected clients and removes its specifications, feedback, sessions, and linked sign-in identities.

Cloudflare also handles technical connection and invocation information. Its Workers Logs retention depends on the account plan and settings; Cloudflare currently documents a maximum of three days on its Free plan and seven days on its Paid plan. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to respond, review relevant feedback, and meet applicable legal requirements. Private feedback records expire 180 days after submission and are removed by an hourly cleanup. You can delete a private feedback record sooner by asking the plugin to delete it and providing its feedback reference.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of processing, and a copy of data you provided. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise a right concerning private plugin feedback, use the feedback reference with the plugin's delete option or email support. To exercise another right or ask a privacy question, email support@bleavit.com. We may ask for information needed to verify and handle your request. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, including the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, or the authority where you live or work.
