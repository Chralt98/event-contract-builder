# Context and background specification

Help an informed non-specialist understand the event without predicting it or
changing its approved interpretation.

- `background` contains relevant durable historical, institutional,
  procedural, or domain context.
- `keyFactors` lists observable developments that could affect the outcome
  without assigning probabilities.
- Optional `references` contain distinct public URLs with title and publisher
  only when they materially support the explanation.

Prefer authoritative primary sources for institutional facts and reputable
secondary sources when they materially improve explanation. Describe disputed
claims accurately. Do not repeat resolution criteria, advocate, offer trading
advice, claim an outcome, or include transient status/news updates.

Background references are explanatory and non-binding: they never replace,
reorder, or join the approved resolution-source hierarchy. Keep the exact
selected unit and every approved upstream stage unchanged.
